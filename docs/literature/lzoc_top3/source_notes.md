## Selection

The working material was identified from the current project as Li₁.₇₅ZrCl₄.₇₅O₀.₅, not Li₃YCl₆ or LiNbOCl₄. Search terms included the exact formula, Li–Zr–O–Cl, amorphous zirconium oxychlorides, and structural/AIMD studies. Priority was given to primary Zr-based research with either composition-matched experiments or directly useful structural methods. “Top three” describes project relevance, not an exhaustive review or citation-count ranking.

## Numeric checks and exclusions

- P1 target: 2.42 mS/cm at 25 °C. Relative pellet density 94.2% at 300 MPa is not g/cm³.
- P2: Li₃ZrCl₄O₁.₅, not the target formula. Conductivity 1.35 ± 0.07 mS/cm; impedance activation energy 0.294 ± 0.003 eV.
- P3 experimental oxygen mixture: 0.8Li₂O–ZrCl₄, nominal Li₁.₆ZrCl₄O₀.₈; 1.78 mS/cm at 25 °C. Important AIMD composition: Li₂.₅ZrCl₅.₅O₀.₅.
- P3 AIMD: NVT, 600–900 K, 2 ps heating, 300 ps production, 2 fs step, thermostat period 80 fs. No unverified discrete temperature list added.
- English and Japanese versions use the same quantities and composition assignments.
- No current MACE accuracy, successful amorphous preparation, or converged diffusion is inferred solely from these papers.
- No exact diffusion coefficient is inferred from experimental conductivity without a stated carrier density and correlation approximation.

The dedicated academic-search connector was unavailable in this session. Publisher search/full text, author-hosted full text, Crossref and PubMed supplied the evidence. This limitation does not justify substituting secondary summaries for primary data.

P1 and P3 identify a Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 license. Reports provide attributed, original summaries. P2 is an ACS publication; only a concise paraphrase, bibliographic metadata and links are provided. No full PDFs, publisher figures or supplementary datasets are uploaded. This is a focused main-text reading, not a claim to have audited every supplementary file.
