All ten figure families replace the previous graphics at the same filenames, in PNG/PDF/SVG. No parallel old-style copies are retained in this folder; Git history preserves the previous versions.

Li₃YCl₆ presentation typography: titles 22 pt, axes 19 pt, ticks/legends 15 pt, summary lines 3 pt, black borders 1.8 pt, frameless legends and grid alpha 0.25. Dense raw thermo lines use 1.2 pt. Subplot canvases are enlarged consistently and long titles/legend labels wrap without changing meaning. PNG exports use 300 dpi; SVG/PDF retain vector text.

This is a presentation-only change. No smoothing, trajectory scaling or fit selection is added. Data and numerical summaries are unchanged; the existing English/Japanese reports automatically reference the replacements. Independent structure and transport limitations in those reports still apply.
