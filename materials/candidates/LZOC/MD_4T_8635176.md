## Protocol

LAMMPS metal units; timestep 0.0005 ps = 0.5 fs. MACE-MPA-0 ML-IAP/Kokkos.
Preserve source cell and coordinates; assign seeded 300 K velocities, remove
initial total momentum. PBC in all directions. No new minimization of the
already prepared structure, and no rescaling of diffusion trajectories.

1. 10 ps NVT ramp from 300 K to target: 20,000 steps, Tdamp=0.1 ps.
2. 50 ps NPT at target, isotropic 1 bar, Tdamp=0.1 ps, Pdamp=1 ps:
   100,000 steps. Cell angles remain as in the initial structure.
3. 200 ps NVT at target: 400,000 steps. Cell fixed to terminal NPT cell,
   not a mean-volume cell. Thermostat reinitialized; velocities retained.

Each phase has its own log and trajectory; time resets to zero at each phase.
Production sampling: thermo/MSD every 0.05 ps; atom dump every 0.1 ps.
Atom dump includes ID, element, wrapped/unwrapped coordinates and image flags.
MSD is single-origin Li-group MSD with Li COM removal, with x/y/z/total columns
in Å²; its step column converts to ps by multiplying by 0.0005.
This is a diagnostic output, not an automatically accepted diffusion fit.
Subsequent analysis must specify COM convention, fitting range and convergence.
Binary restart checkpoints every 10 ps in NPT and production.
